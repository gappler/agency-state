I help brand marketing teams and their agencies use AI in Claude Code and Codex to produce marketing work they can stand behind. I partner with them to build a system I call Brand OS. It turns a brand into one source AI can read, serves that source to the team’s automations and agents, and helps the team make better calls about which AI work represents its brand and is ready to ship. Brand OS

In a test with Yowie, a demo outdoor-gear brand, Claude was asked for a cooler Yowie doesn't make and wrote a full product page for it. The page passed every rule check; only a check against the product list caught it. Voice is a separate problem. Recent research found that AI models given a brand's voice to imitate still write in their own voice, not the brand's (Victor A. Barger, working paper, 2026, tested on three Claude models and one brand).

Brand OS builds checks against the brand source into a team's work and sends every draft to a person for review.

Background

I've spent more than twenty years leading brand marketing strategy, operations, and teams. Most recently, a decade at The American Institute of Architects as Managing Director of Brand & Strategic Marketing, where I led record-breaking membership and conference campaigns, modernized marketing operations, and built a 12-person team. I was the business owner for a brand redesign adopted across the national organization and its chapter network, with visual identity by Pentagram. Before the AIA, I spent six years as VP of Digital Marketing at GRAFIK, an agency in Alexandria, Virginia, on B2B clients including Autodesk and Accenture. Earlier, Designory on Nissan and Infiniti, Young & Rubicam on Lincoln Mercury, and Bacardi Global Brands in-house. I've worked agency-side or in-house on brand clients my whole career.

For the past two years, I've been building with Claude Code and Codex. I'm currently developing Agency State's BrandBuilder, the tool behind Brand OS. It turns a brand's written and visual guidelines into a reviewed source for AI tools and runs the checks and routing around it. I've used it on Agency State's own brand, which is now served to my AI tools, and I'm testing it on demo brands before I use it with a client.
