Agency State

Agency State is my practice. I help marketing leaders who are building their brand marketing workspace with Claude Code, Codex, and their own agents. Of course these tools don't know a brand until they do, and that's the work: getting the written and visual brand into one source that the team owns, the whole stack works from, and every decision improves. I call this Brand OS.

The published research and my own testing agree: no model follows a brand on its own. A person stays in the loop, and the system's job is to spend human attention only where it's needed. The practice pages in the next section discuss my approach.

I've worked at the intersection of brand marketing, operations, and martech for more than two decades. Most recently, a decade at The American Institute of Architects as Managing Director of Brand & Strategic Marketing, where I modernized marketing operations, built a 12-person team, and was business owner for a brand redesign adopted at the national organization and across the chapter network, with messaging strategy by marketing staff and visual identity in collaboration with Pentagram. Earlier brands include Nissan, Infiniti, and Bacardi Global Brands. See my LinkedIn for details.
