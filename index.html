Greg Appler

I've spent more than twenty years leading brand marketing operations and teams. Now I help those teams put AI to work.

Agency State

Agency State works with brand marketing leaders and the builders on their teams as they create their own AI workflows with Claude Code and Codex. The system I build with them is Brand OS. It turns the brand into a source AI tools can read, keeps the team's AI work on brand, and improves as it works with the team in production.

With Brand OS, automations and agents draft and adapt the team's recurring marketing work from the brand source. Checks catch specific errors, and people review each draft and make the brand decisions. When the work reveals a gap, the team approves changes to the source, checks, or review process. I start by assessing the existing brand material, the decisions to make, and which work to build first.

Background

During a decade at The American Institute of Architects as Managing Director of Brand & Strategic Marketing, I led record-breaking membership and conference campaigns, modernized marketing operations, and built a 12-person team. I managed the development of AIAU, the AIA's first on-demand continuing education platform. I was also the business owner for a brand redesign adopted across the national organization and its chapter network, with visual identity by Pentagram. Before the AIA, I was a digital marketing lead on Nissan, Infiniti, and Bacardi Global Brands.

I'm a builder at heart, and for the past two years I've been building with Claude Code, Codex, and other automation tools. I'm developing Agency State's BrandBuilder to turn a brand's written and visual guidelines into a source AI can work from. As is often the case, it's a bigger problem than I thought, but I'm testing it on demo brands and it's nearly ready for client work.

I think real automation gains have to include the work between the work: the packaging, explaining, approvals, and feedback that don't follow a set order and take up much of a marketing project's time. I'm working on a campaign engine where agents run each phase of a campaign, then stop and hand a person the few decisions that need their judgment. I'm testing it on a fictional trade association. I have other products on the boards, from small to ambitious.

I'm also open to work where my marketing and building experience fits. If you'd like to work together, send me an email.
