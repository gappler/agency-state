Agency State

Agency State builds Brand OS for brand marketing teams: the codified brand, plus the automations, agents, checks, and feedback loops that run on it.

The work starts with the brand a team already has. Brand books, voice rules, visual systems, product facts, claims, approved language, and prior work become structured source the team can review, revise, and own. That source is the foundation. Without it, an agent can sound close to the brand and still invent a claim, drop a required condition, or follow an old decision.

Once the source exists, it can be served into the work. Automations and agents use it to draft, adapt, check, and route marketing work across the team's own stack. Mechanical rules get checked by the system. Decisions that need judgment go to the person who owns them. Approved corrections go back into the source, so the Brand OS improves as the team uses it.

I build this with marketing leaders who are already using Claude Code, Codex, and their own agents, and who need the brand to hold as AI takes on more of the work.

Inside Brand OS
01

Source

The codified brand: the team's decided material turned into structured source, with provenance, gaps, conflicts, and open decisions made visible.

02

Production

Automations and agents make the team's recurring work: drafts, adaptations, and channel versions. The brief and the idea come from the team; the source supplies the brand side.

03

Control

Checks test what a machine can verify, such as product facts, claim conditions, banned words, and visual rules. What needs judgment routes to a person.

04

Feedback

Corrections return to the source, the rules, or the review path, so repeated catches become settled guidance instead of the same review burden returning.

05

Operators

The people who own the brand's decisions keep the system current: approving exceptions, retiring old facts, settling open questions, and deciding what can ship.

The first build

BrandBuilder is the codification workflow I am productizing first. It uses a baseline inventory to read existing brand material, identify what is ready to codify, and produce structured brand source for the team to review.

The useful output is not a prettier brand book. It is reviewed source the Brand OS can run on: structured brand files, provenance, gaps, conflicts, and the decisions the team needs to settle before the source becomes authoritative.

From there, the Brand OS expands into the production loop: agents that make the work, checks that test the facts and rules, routes for human judgment, and a feedback process that keeps the source current.
