A codified brand template

The 46 entries I start from when preparing a brand for AI tools.

The baseline

This is the baseline I start from when preparing a brand for AI tools: 46 entries, 34 required, 11 recommended, and one optional. It's a template, not a closed list. A brand can add entries its work needs, and entries that don't apply are marked not applicable and set aside. During codification, each entry is filled from the brand's existing material. What the material doesn't settle goes to the person who decides it.

assessment status: filled: current, sourced, ready to codify partial: exists, but stale, scattered, or unsourced empty: the brand has not decided n/a: this brand does not need it

Identity

01 required brand-name usage the approved name and how to use it 02 required meaning of the name what the name means and any interpretation the brand explicitly rules out 03 required standard brand lines the approved tagline, positioning line, and short preview line 04 recommended values the brand's stated values 05 recommended mission or purpose the mission or purpose, if the brand uses one

Brand architecture and naming

06 recommended product and sub-brand structure how product lines and sub-brands relate to the company and each other 07 recommended capitalization capitalization of brand, product, and sub-brand names 08 recommended names that must not be abbreviated names that must always appear in full

Positioning and audience

09 required positioning statement what the brand offers and the premise behind it 10 required audience who the audience is, what they already know, and how to address them 11 required category the brand's category 12 required differentiators what makes the brand distinct, with evidence for its claims 13 optional competitor information supported, verifiable facts needed for an appropriate comparison

Brand perspectives

14 recommended chosen points of view adopted brand views, with an owner and date for each

Voice

15 required preferred words words the brand favors and guidance for using them 16 required words to avoid words the brand prohibits or refuses, with reasons 17 required sentence length and rhythm how sentences should read and connect 18 required reading level language complexity for the audience 19 required person when to use I, we, you, or third-person references 20 required punctuation the brand's punctuation preferences and restrictions 21 required formatting instructions for paragraphs, headings, lists, and other text formatting 22 required style guide the style guide of record and any house exceptions 23 required whole-passage voice examples at least three sourced voice passages, preserved word for word 24 recommended levels of formality when to use a relaxed, formal, or otherwise different register 25 recommended channel differences how the core voice changes for particular channels or uses

Visual identity

26 required color palette exact color values and the role of each color 27 required typefaces families, weights, uses, and the character the type should convey 28 required logo usage approved logo files and instructions for using them 29 required spacing and layout guidance for arranging content and using space 30 required imagery and illustration image and illustration direction, with references where supplied 31 required iconography icon style, color, size, set, and where icons belong

Products, facts, and claims

32 required product list and status product names and lines, identifying information, and active or retired status 33 required product facts specifications and other supported facts that copy may state 34 required prices or pricing responsibility supported amounts and currencies, or the role supplying prices 35 required approved claims product-specific or catalog-wide claims, with their stated conditions

Approved language

36 required company descriptions approved 25-word, 50-word, 100-word, and full descriptions, with usage instructions 37 required legal and disclosure language wording to retain, with instructions for where and when it applies 38 required attributions required credits and attribution wording 39 recommended reusable passages approved passages, their sources, and permission to reuse or adapt

Stories

40 recommended story library sourced origin stories, case examples, or lessons learned, tagged by subject, audience, and length as appropriate

Rules

41 required never-say rules the active prohibitions used by the checks, consistent with the voice's words to avoid 42 required claim restrictions rules about what may be claimed and under which conditions. Approved claim wording remains with entry 35 43 required formatting rules the formatting requirements to check or send for review, consistent with entry 21 44 recommended words allowed under conditions words permitted only under recorded conditions

Decision owners and review

45 required decision owners who has authority over voice, claims, visual identity, and legal review. Add other responsibilities where needed 46 required review record interpretations, conflicts, gaps, proposed decisions, and decisions recorded during review
